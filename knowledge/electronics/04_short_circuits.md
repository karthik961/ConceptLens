# Short circuits and safe reasoning

**Concept notes written for the ConceptLens starter demo.**

A short circuit is an unintended very low-resistance path between points of different potential, or a path that bypasses a load. It can cause a large current limited by the actual resistance and internal resistance of the source.

A short circuit is different from an open circuit. An open circuit interrupts a path so current cannot cross the gap. A short circuit may offer an easier unintended path and can cause overheating or damage. Never short a real battery to test a hypothesis.

Circuit diagrams distinguish components, connection nodes, and switches. When reasoning about a circuit, trace the current path from one source terminal through components and back to the other terminal.
