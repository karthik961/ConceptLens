# Resistance and Ohm’s law

**ConceptLens original teaching note; demonstration content, not a vetted textbook extract.**

Resistance describes how strongly a component opposes current. For an ohmic component under fixed conditions, voltage, current, and resistance satisfy V = I × R. At the same voltage, greater resistance means less current. This relation does not say that every component has a fixed resistance: a filament lamp changes resistance as it heats.
