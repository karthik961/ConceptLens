# Conductors, insulators and charge

**Concept notes written for the ConceptLens starter demo.**

Metals generally conduct because some electrons can move through the material. Insulators resist charge motion under ordinary conditions. Whether a bulb lights depends on both the material in the loop and whether the path is complete.

Electrons are not created by a light bulb, and a lamp does not consume electrons as if they were fuel. Electrical energy supplied by the source is converted to heat and light in the lamp.

An open air gap is usually insulating at ordinary low voltages, but extremely high voltages can cause dielectric breakdown. Basic circuit questions usually assume a simple ideal opening that prevents current.
